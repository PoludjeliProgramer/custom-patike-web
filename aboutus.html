Our Story

Custom Patike is an atelier built on the discipline of hand-painting. Every sneaker is hand-painted on an authentic base sourced from official suppliers — 40+ hours of brushwork per pair. We don't print. We don't stencil. We build wearable art, one piece at a time.

Hand-Painted Footwear.

Every pair begins as a blank canvas. We meticulously prepare each authentic sneaker, hand-mix our paints, and apply the custom artwork brush by brush. Sealed with an industrial-grade, UV-resistant varnish, your sneakers are fully waterproof, crack-proof, and completely ready for daily wear.
